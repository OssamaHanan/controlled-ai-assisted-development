# Dataset Summary

## Overview

This research is based on a small empirical case study of AI-assisted development in **NeuroNova**, a student-built AI learning platform.

The dataset contains **16 recorded AI-assisted development experiments**.

## Dataset Size

| Category | Count |
|---|---:|
| Total experiments | 16 |
| Controlled experiments | 10 |
| Uncontrolled experiments | 6 |
| Successful experiments | 13 |
| Failed or rejected experiments | 3 |

## Success Rates

| Workflow Type | Successful | Total | Success Rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Controlled AI-assisted development | 9 | 10 | 90% |
| Uncontrolled AI-assisted coding | 4 | 6 | 66.7% |
