# AI Use Disclosure

This research project studies AI-assisted software development and also used AI tools during parts of the research and writing process.

AI tools were used to support:

- brainstorming research structure
- improving grammar and wording
- organizing Markdown files
- summarizing experiment notes
- preparing public research summaries
- checking clarity and consistency
- improving explanations of the controlled workflow

The author remained responsible for:

- selecting the research topic
- building and maintaining the NeuroNova project
- recording the experiments
- interpreting the results
- deciding which outputs to accept or reject
- checking dataset consistency
- revising the paper
- writing final claims carefully
- ensuring that the research does not exaggerate its findings

AI assistance was used as a support tool, not as an independent researcher or author.

The research argues that AI tools should be used with human oversight, review, and responsibility. The same principle was followed during the preparation of this project.
